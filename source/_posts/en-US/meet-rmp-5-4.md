Rail Map Painter 5.4 is here! This update brings fill nodes, rotate & flip, a line style panel, automatic transfer info fill, three new city styles, and mobile experience improvements.

---

## 🎨 Fill Nodes

Draw colored areas on your map to represent parks, water bodies, and other geographic features. Customize color, opacity, and choose from built-in tree and water patterns. Drag corner nodes to freely adjust the shape.

<video src="/rmt-blog/images/rmp-5-4-fill-node.mp4" autoplay loop muted playsinline style="max-width: 100%;"></video>

---

## 🔄 Rotate & Flip

Select multiple elements, then rotate or flip all selected parts at once — no need to reposition them one by one.

<video src="/rmt-blog/images/rmp-5-4-rotate-flip.mp4" autoplay loop muted playsinline style="max-width: 100%;"></video>

---

## 🎛️ Line Style Selection Panel

Line styles can now be selected before drawing a segment. Don't forget to confirm in the left panel before linking segments!

![Line Style Selection Panel](/rmt-blog/images/rmp-5-4-line-style-panel.png)

---

## 🔗 Automatic Transfer Info Fill

When multiple lines converge at the same station, the system automatically switches the station type to a transfer station and fills in the line colors.

<video src="/rmt-blog/images/rmp-5-4-transfer-autofill.mp4" autoplay loop muted playsinline style="max-width: 100%;"></video>

## 📱 Mobile Experience Improvements

### Radial Touch Menu

A fan-shaped popup menu centered on the station node, designed specifically for touch interaction.

![Radial Touch Menu](/rmt-blog/images/rmp-5-4-radial-menu.jpeg)

### Virtual Joystick Input

Directional keys + delete/copy buttons for precise node movement control.

![Virtual Joystick Input](/rmt-blog/images/rmp-5-4-joystick.jpeg)
